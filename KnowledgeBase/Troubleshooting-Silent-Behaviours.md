# Troubleshooting Silent Behaviours

This page covers the things Rocket Slicer does without any message: fibre that silently disappears, features missing in Fortified, edits that do not stick, settings that do nothing, fans and heaters that behave unexpectedly, and version-specific bugs seen in real exported G-code. Most of these look like faults but are the slicer behaving as designed; the rest are bugs, with the community issue linked where there is one. If the slicer did show a message, see [Troubleshooting slicer messages](Troubleshooting-Slicer-Messages.md) instead.

*Applies to: Rocket Slicer 1.4.0.857 (1.3.1.480 where stated); printer firmware 2.2.42.831.320.*

Settings are given as UI label (`SettingKey`). ↑ means raise, ↓ means lower. S / R / F = Speedy / Reinforced / Fortified. Factory values are from the group "PETG 0.2 mm | CFC PETG+X-CCF / PETG" unless stated. None of the findings below has yet been checked against physical prints.

## Silent behaviours: geometry and fibre

**Fibre goes only where a strand is long enough to cut and restart; everything else is dropped or turned into plastic, without a message.** Check the preview ("Show Fiber Only", Line Type colours) before printing. See [Fibre paths](Fibre-Paths.md) for the full rules.

| Symptom | Cause | What to do | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| No fibre at all | Speedy mode (fibre forced off); or the Shell / Infill fibre switches (`GenerateFiberPerimeters` / `GenerateFiberInfill`) are off; or a mask or layup turned fibre off in that region | Use Reinforced or Fortified; check the switches and masks | Behaviour analysis |
| No fibre loop on small parts, small islands or small holes | The joined strand is shorter than ≈ 58 mm (the cut distance), or a loop is shorter than 55 mm (Minimum perimeter length). A round hole needs a diameter of ≈ 17.5 mm at the loop line | Minimum perimeter length ↓ (it cannot beat the 58 mm cut floor, and the tooltip warns of poor lay-up); F. Outer ↑ lengthens the joined outer strand; enlarge holes | Behaviour analysis (arithmetic inferred) |
| No fibre in thin walls | No room for Outer wall + P. Outer + one 0.8 mm fibre loop | P. Outer (`InsetXPOuterWithFiberCount`) ↓ to 0–1; Outer wall (`Inset0Count`) ↓; thicken the wall in CAD | Inferred |
| Fibre infill missing in small regions | Infill strands shorter than ≈ 63 mm (cut distance + 5 mm) are dropped; solid lines link only across gaps ≤ 4.8 mm (6 × line width). On a Benchy, factory Reinforced Isogrid at 20 % (and 10 %) lays **no** fibre infill; 30 % lays 5 strands, 60–80 % lays 63–68 ([#43](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/43)) | Raise the density (Enhancement Magnitude 60–80 %), or accept it; check the preview | Behaviour analysis; G-code (1.3.1, shortest strand 70.4 mm) |
| Plastic where fibre was expected (Reinforced) | Fibre loops that are rejected are replaced by "Internal solid infill"; short fibre infill stubs become plastic | Normal; see the rows above to get fibre there | Behaviour analysis |
| **Fortified features missing or printed in mid-air**: thin walls, pillars, towers, pins, small holes, and whatever sits on them | 1.4.0 has no plastic fallback: whatever fibre cannot fill is left empty, with no message. A factory Fortified Benchy lost its cabin walls and window pillars (Z ≈ 25.5–31.8 mm) and its chimney, and its roof was sliced over nothing. 1.3.1 filled such areas with FFF plastic (103 of 240 Benchy layers plastic only), as the official manual describes, so this is a regression ([#38](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/38)) | Check the preview layer by layer before printing. Use Reinforced for parts with thin walls, towers or pins, or thicken those features *(inferred)*. Quick tell in the file: a Z jump of more than one macro layer between consecutive `; LAYER:` markers, or `LAYER_COUNT` larger than `TOTAL_LAYER` (a hint only) | G-code (1.4.0, 1.3.1); Community [#38](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/38) |
| Fortified outer surface rough or slightly off-size | There is no plastic outer wall: the surface is the fibre bead, and accuracy depends on trajectory correction | Expected; use Reinforced for a plastic skin | Behaviour analysis + Inferred |
| Fibre only on every second output layer | Fibre is laid once per macro layer; plastic prints in micro layers (factory R PETG: outer wall at macro ÷ 2) | Expected | Behaviour analysis |
| Solid fibre all in one direction | Guide directions (`InfillFSolidAngleList`) is "0" in all Reinforced Solid profiles and in 7 of 16 Fortified profiles | Set "0/90" or "0/60/-60" | G-code (1.4.0 cube: 84 of 84 fibre-infill layers at 0°); Community [#47](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/47) |
| Fibre direction changed inside a mask or layup | New masks and layups force Guide directions to "0/45/-45/90" | Edit the rule's directions | Behaviour analysis |
| A mask or layup is ignored | A part has masks **or** layups, never both: entering Layup mode turns masks off on every part. A new mask gets the lowest priority and loses overlaps. Drawn masks span the full part height | Re-enable masks; raise the priority; move or scale the mask in Z | Behaviour analysis |
| Support appears after switching to Reinforced | Enable support (`GenerateSupport`) is on in every factory Reinforced profile | Turn it off if the part does not need it | Behaviour analysis |
| "Build plate only" looks inverted | The switch shows the inverse of the stored `GenerateInternalSupport`; factory profiles show "off", so support may start on the model | Turn it on for plate-only support | Behaviour analysis (support-on-model inferred) |
| Painted fibre seam seems to work although the panel says plastic only | The panel text is stale; "Seam Fibers" strokes are used for fibre seams | Use it | Behaviour analysis |
| Auto-orient puts the load along Z | Auto orient minimises support only; it knows nothing about fibre, which lies only in XY | Orient manually for the load paths | Behaviour analysis + Inferred |
| Small parts print slowly | Minimum layer print time (`MinLayerTimeForSlowing`, 10 s) scales every extrusion move | Print several parts together, or ↓ the value | Behaviour analysis |
| Small layers still too fast or overheating (1.3.1) | The 1.3.1 slowdown stopped at ≈ 40 mm/s, leaving layers far under target ([#5](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/5)); 1.4.0 adds a minimum-speed setting, untested in output | More parts per plate; more cooling | G-code (1.3.1); Community [#5](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/5) |

## Silent behaviours: settings, preview and machine

**Before blaming a setting, make sure the edit reached the G-code; then check the machine-side commands the slicer adds on its own.**

| Symptom | Cause | What to do | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edits don't stick** after reopening | Sidebar edits to a factory ("AP") template live only in memory; on reopen the factory row is restored | Duplicate the group and edit the copy | Behaviour analysis (outcome *untested*) |
| A dialog edit has no effect | Factory template: view-only, no Apply. Custom template: nothing applies until Apply, and a red field makes Apply fail silently | Duplicate; press Apply; fix red fields | Behaviour analysis |
| An edit has no effect in one mode | Each mode is its own profile. Fortified forces plastic walls, skins and sparse infill to 0 and fibre infill to Solid; Speedy forces fibre off | Edit the mode you print; see the forced list in [How the slicer works](How-the-Slicer-Works.md) | Behaviour analysis |
| A mask value has no effect | The mask form has no limits; out-of-range values are probably replaced by the built-in default | Keep values within the profile's range | Inferred |
| A setting appears inert | Adaptive-wall fields (ignored); Do coast / Do wipe nozzle (their lengths are 0 in every factory plastic); Extra unretract on tool change (needs the printer's "Do unretract before tool change", which is off); jerk fields (jerk control off); Correction move speed (needs "Override correction move speed", which is off, so the Reinforced 0.1 mm/s value is inert *(inferred)*) | See [Settings overview](Settings-Overview.md) for settings that don't do what they say | Behaviour analysis |
| **Enhancement Magnitude shows the wrong step** | The slider opens at step 1 (10 %) whatever the profile holds (factory 20–25 %); re-picking the step that was last written writes nothing; keyboard Enter/Space writes nothing | Move to another step and back with the mouse; check the density in the dialog | Behaviour analysis; Community [#40](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/40) |
| Sidebar "Printing Speed" or "Layer Height" seems wrong | "Printing Speed" writes only the inner-wall speed; "Layer Height" is read-only (the outer-wall micro-layer height, 0.12 mm in factory R PETG) | Edit the specific fields in the dialog | Behaviour analysis |
| Raising plastic speed saves no time | A hidden 25 mm³/s volumetric cap (1.4.0). Cap speed = 25 ÷ (width × height × flow multiplier), e.g. ≈ 258 mm/s for Speedy 0.2 mm sparse infill (0.44 × 0.2 × 1.1) and ≈ 231 mm/s for Reinforced X-CCF infill | Expected | Behaviour analysis |
| **Print takes longer than the estimate** | Initial heat-up is charged ≈ 3.5–5.4 s; each tool change is charged ≈ 5–6 s against ≈ 18–41 s of real heating; brush, cutter and strand macros are charged ≈ 0. The motion estimate itself is not optimistic (8–31 % above a Klipper-style model). Reinforced Benchys are under-estimated by ≈ 14–31 % (upper bound; [#41](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/41)) | Treat the estimate (and the time in the file name) as a lower bound; add ≈ 45–60 s per macro layer in Reinforced. See [Print time](Print-Time.md) | G-code (1.3.1) + Inferred |
| Tooltip and behaviour disagree | Preheat "before the extruder change" is written only seconds before `M109` on this printer; the Internal solid infill tooltip says "bottom solid skin"; the compensation-ratio tooltips don't say they apply to the composite head only; tooltip illustrations are not shown in 1.4.0 and many are outdated (cut 13 mm vs 58 mm) | Trust the behaviour, not the tooltip | Behaviour analysis (compensation scope inferred); Community [#41](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/41) |
| **Fibre runs out before the preview says it should** | Reported fibre length, weight and cost leave out the 55 mm restart feed of every strand: real tow use is 1.24–1.98 × the reported figure (1.3.1 Benchys) ([#39](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/39)) | Add 55 mm × the number of strands (`M1001` lines) | G-code (1.3.1) |
| **Cost ≈ 0 for the fibre-composite matrix** | Matrix cost is 1 per kg in CFC PETG+X-CCF and CFC PA+X-CCF; PLA X-CCF fibre is 60 vs 180 elsewhere; the currency setting only changes the symbol | Enter real prices in the composites | Behaviour analysis |
| Composite length shown in "mm" in the side panel | The value is in metres (label error) | Read it as metres | Inferred |
| **Chamber stops heating mid-print** | Any "Auxiliary cooling fan" row (`M106 P3`; 28 of the 30 factory R/F fan tables run it at 50–100 % from layer 2–5) switches the chamber heater off and opens the louvres: auxiliary cooling and chamber heating are mutually exclusive (per the manufacturer in [#35](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/35)) | For chamber-heated jobs, clear the auxiliary column in a copied profile *(inferred)* | Behaviour analysis; Community [#35](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/35) (manufacturer statement) |
| **Fans left on after the print** | The aux and exhaust values (`M106 P3` / `P5`) persist and are never switched off; the end code zeroes only `M106 P1`. While P3 is set, the louvres stay open and the heater stays off; the filter fan (P0) stops by itself at the end of the job (per the manufacturer in [#35](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/35)) | Add `M106 P3 S0` and `M106 P5 S0` to the printer End G-code, ending with a newline | G-code (1.3.1, nine files) |
| Brief 100 % fan burst after each tool change | Every tool change ends with `M106 P<new> S255` until the next entity sets the material's value | Expected | Behaviour analysis |
| **Bed temperature flipping** (PPS CF group) | `M140` follows the active head's material: PPS CF 85 °C, CFC PA 80 °C, never waited on (no real file pairs different bed temperatures, so untested) | Give both materials the same bed temperature | Behaviour analysis; Community [#45](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/45) |
| First layer printed on a bed that is still heating | The start waits (`M190`) on the regular bed value; a higher first-layer bed value is then set at layer 1 without a wait ([#44](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/44)) | Set first-layer bed = regular bed, or add `M190 S<first-layer value>` to the printer Start G-code | G-code (1.3.1) |
| Chamber switches off at start | **Default behaviour:** Has heated chamber is effectively on (the preset's "off" override is not applied) and every factory material holds Chamber temperature 0, so every job sends `M141 S0` and `M191 S0` at the start and `M141 S0` at the end. `M141 S0` switches the heater off when a target is set (per the manufacturer in [#35](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/35)), so a chamber preheated from the panel is cancelled *(inferred; untested)* | Set the material chamber temperatures (copy the material first) | G-code (1.3.1 and 1.4.0) + Inferred |
| **Pressure advance not applied** (Speedy "PET GF 0.1 mm", both groups) | The Start G-code has `SET PRESSURE ADVANCE` / `SMOOTH TIME` with spaces and split lines; stock Klipper reports "Unknown command" and carries on | Replace with `SET_PRESSURE_ADVANCE EXTRUDER=extruder1 ADVANCE=0.035 SMOOTH_TIME=0.04` | Behaviour analysis (how the vendor firmware reacts is unknown); Community [#42](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/42) |
| Motion feels different between jobs | Klipper keeps pressure advance, minimum cruise ratio (0.8 in R/F) and square-corner velocity until restart, so a profile that omits a line inherits the last job's value. The composite head's pressure advance is never set by 1.4.0 presets. Gap infill (1.4.0) and overhang infill (1.3.1) run at 8000 mm/s², everything else at 5000 | Set every value explicitly in each custom profile's Start G-code. See [Motion settings](Motion-Settings.md) | Behaviour analysis; G-code (`M204`) |
| **Custom G-code loses its last character** | Each field is trimmed of trailing `\` and lowercase `n` characters (not newlines): `…FAN=chamber_fan` becomes `…chamber_fa` ([#48](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/48)) | End every custom field with a newline or ` ;` | G-code (1.4.0: `M117 fan` written as `M117 fa`) |
| Custom G-code `{placeholders}` printed literally | No placeholders are supported; the profile Start G-code runs first, before heating and tool selection | Use literal values or `printer.cfg` macros. See [Custom G-code and export](Custom-G-code-and-Export.md) | Behaviour analysis |
| Head offset entered, parts misaligned | The slicer's head offsets are 0 because the firmware applies them; a slicer offset would probably be applied twice | Keep `OffsetX` / `OffsetY` / `OffsetZ` at 0. For a shift between heads, run nozzle-offset calibration, then correct the machine X/Y offset on the printer | Inferred; Official |

## Silent behaviours: version-specific bugs seen in real G-code

**These were found by reading real exported files; each row names the slicer version that showed it.** Check the version line at the top of a shared file before applying a row. Every row is *G-code* evidence for the version named.

| Symptom | Version | Cause seen in the G-code | What to do | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top surface sags or shows holes over a large flat roof | 1.4.0 | The bridge layer and top skin are full, but the three solid layers between them are almost empty (Top solid 5) | Inspect large roofs layer by layer in the preview; try more top layers *(untested)* | [#30](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/30); the manufacturer says it is a regression from 1.3.2, fixed for the next hotfix build (not verified) |
| Stringing on long travels | 1.4.0 | A no-op `G1 E0` is written where a retract belongs, followed by up to 33 mm of unretracted travel; the file has Do wipe nozzle on with wipe length 0 *(link inferred)* | Turn Do wipe nozzle off | [#1](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/1) |
| Stringing inside the part | 1.3.1 | Do retract only when crossing borders is on: travels up to 52 mm inside the part carry no retract (longest single move 51.8 mm, line 615 of the `Benchy_renforced_level5.gcode` test file, excluding the move off the brush after a tool change); PLA files also leave 2–3.5 mm travels unretracted against a 2 mm threshold (cause unknown) | Turn that switch off if internal stringing is visible | [#1](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/1) |
| Nozzle scars or extra time from Z-hops with Do Z-hop off | 1.3.1 | Plastic Z-hops emitted regardless (9,655 in the Fortified Benchy, 8,886 of them without a retract) | Nothing to change in the slicer; check 1.4.0 output | [#29](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/29) |
| Plastic flow above what the hot end can melt | 1.3.1 | No volumetric cap: up to 61 mm³/s; 1.4.0 caps at 25 mm³/s | Use 1.4.0, or lower the speeds | [#4](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/4) (a 1.3.1 finding) |
| Seams stacked in one line | both | Corner-seeking seam with distribution off: same spot for 55–124 layers | Paint seams or enable seam distribution | [#2](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/2) (by design) |
| Small gaps (0.6–1.6 mm) between walls | mainly 1.3.1 | Classic walls leave enclosed gaps; 1.4.0 adds variable-width gap infill | Use 1.4.0 and keep gap infill on | [#3](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/3) |
| Fortified job uses the FFF head | 1.3.1 | The header says `Composite Only`, but the file has 136 changes to `T1` | Load FFF plastic too (1.4.0 uses none) | Noted in [#38](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/38) |
| **Fortified drops thin features; parts above are sliced over nothing** | 1.4.0 | One `T0 R`, no `T1`; the Benchy has no extrusion at Z 25.5–31.8 mm or above 40 mm; `; LAYER:` jumps from Z 25.35 to 31.83 | Check the preview; use Reinforced or thicken the features *(inferred)* | [#38](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/38) (critical) |
| G-code rejected with error 10057 / 10009 on a comma-decimal PC | 1.3.x | Comma decimals inside commands (e.g. `P0,05`) | Use 1.4.0 (commands fixed) | [ERROR_CODES.md](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/blob/main/ERROR_CODES.md) |
| Community tools misread `; LAYER:` / `; SEAM` values | 1.4.0 | Comments still use the locale's decimal comma (`[0,2]`) | Tools must accept either separator; printing is unaffected | [#46](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/46) (cosmetic) |
| `LAYER_COUNT` header larger than the printed layers | 1.4.0 | 303 vs 155 (Plastic Only test file); 167 vs 140 (Fortified Benchy, where the 27 extra are the empty Z gap *(inferred)*); `TOTAL_LAYER` is right | Ignore the value; in Fortified, treat it as a hint of dropped features and check the preview | Minor; part of [#38](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/38) |

**Evidence.** The 1.3.1.480 Benchys and the 1.4.0.857 Plastic Only job are in the repo's [Test_files](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/tree/main/Test_files). The 1.4.0.857 Fortified Benchy (factory "PETG 0.2 mm | CFC PETG+X-CCF / PETG", repo `3DBenchy.stl`), the 1.4.0 cube and the `M117` test are described in [#38](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/38), [#47](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/47) and [#48](https://github.com/3ricj/FibreSeeker3/issues/48).
